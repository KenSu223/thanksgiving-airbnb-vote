# Artwork

- `cat.webp`: the fishing cat. いらすとや's 「猫の模様のイラスト（サバ白）」
  (a grey tabby with a white belly and white paws, close to an American
  Shorthair with white), by みふねたかし:
  https://www.irasutoya.com/2018/12/blog-post_505.html
  Downloaded on 2026-10-19 from the 800 px PNG, cropped to the cat and
  re-encoded as a 300 px tall WebP; nothing else changed. いらすとや's terms
  (https://www.irasutoya.com/p/terms.html) allow free use, personal or
  commercial, up to 20 illustrations in one work, and editing; credit isn't
  required, but it's given here. The copyright stays with the artist, so the
  image isn't to be redistributed as material on its own.

Everything else in 小小钓鱼 (the boat, the rod, the sea, every fish and the
图鉴 icons) is drawn in code in `web/src/features/fishing/draw.js`.
